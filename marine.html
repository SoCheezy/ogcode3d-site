How it works

From the part to the ship
  1. Send what you haveA photo with a ruler, a drawing, the old part or just its dimensions.
  2. We model itWe rebuild the part in CAD, and can strengthen it where the old one wore out. The model stays on file, so the next one is quicker.
  3. Quote and lead timeYou get the material, price and delivery date before anything is made.
  4. Print, finish, deliverPrinted in metal or polymer, finished, and delivered to the yard or the office.

Why print it

Spare parts on demand

No moulds, no minimums

One part is as easy to make as twenty. Order exactly what the ship needs, or keep a few in stock.

Parts that no longer exist

When a maker has stopped supplying a part, we make it from the old one.

Metal and polymer

Metal for parts that take load and heat, engineering polymers for covers, housings, guards and fittings.

Engineer-led

Run by a mechanical engineer (NTUA) who installs and runs industrial metal 3D printers across Europe. More about us →
