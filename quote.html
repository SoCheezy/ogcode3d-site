Start a print

Drop in your 3D models for an instant price. We measure each part and estimate the cost for the material and delivery you pick. Your files stay on your computer until you send the request.

Example prices while we finish setting up. Your confirmed quote may differ.

1 · Your models

What kind of model? A 3D model of your object saved as an .stl file, the standard file for 3D printing. Most 3D designs can be saved this way.

Where to find one? Download ready-made models from Printables, Thingiverse or MakerWorld, or export your own design from any 3D software with File → Export → STL.

No model, or a different file? Send a photo, a sketch or a STEP, OBJ or 3MF file through Start a project and we take it from there.
