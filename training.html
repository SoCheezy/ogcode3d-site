Training

Lessons in Computer-Aided Design (CAD), Design for Additive Manufacturing (DfAM) and 3D printing, taught by a mechanical engineer who uses them every day. Learn to model in 3D, design parts that print well and run a printer with confidence. Learn by making: every lesson works toward a real part you take home.

Fusion 360 · Materialise Magics
1-to-1 or groups · Athens or online

What you can learn

Fusion 360 · Magics · AM

CAD & 3D modelling

Fusion 360 from the first sketch to a print-ready model: parametric parts, assemblies, freeform shapes and drawings.

Design for additive manufacturing

Design for the process: orientation, supports, tolerances, lattices, choosing material and technology, and data preparation in Materialise Magics.

3D printing in practice

Slicing, printer setup and calibration, materials, post-processing and troubleshooting, hands-on with real printers.

Common questions

Do I need experience?
No. Lessons start from your level, from first steps to advanced techniques.
Which software do you teach?
Mainly Fusion 360 for design and CAD, and Materialise Magics for preparing files for industrial 3D printing. Using something else? Tell us and we'll see if it fits.
Do I need my own printer?
No. In-person lessons use our printers, and online lessons can include prints we make for you.
What does it cost?
It depends on the subject, the number of lessons and the format. You get a price before the first lesson.
