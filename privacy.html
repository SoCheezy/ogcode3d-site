Who is responsible

Sotirios Vlogiannitis, trading as OGCODE, Karaoli kai Dimitriou 14, 1st floor, 190 02 Paiania, East Attica, Greece, is responsible for the personal data collected through this site. For anything about your data, write to ogcode3d@gmail.com.

What is collected and why

WhenWhatWhy, and the legal basis
You send a form (Start a print, Start a project, Training, Software)Name, email, and anything you add: phone, Instagram handle, city, your message, links and attached files such as STL models or photosTo reply, prepare a quote and do the work you ask for. Steps before a contract and the contract itself (GDPR art. 6(1)(b))
You place an orderName, email, phone, shipping address, what you ordered. Card and bank details go straight to Viva.com and are never seen or stored by usTo make and deliver your order (art. 6(1)(b)) and to keep invoices as tax law requires (art. 6(1)(c))
You email or message usYour contact details and the conversationTo answer you (art. 6(1)(b) or our legitimate interest in replying, art. 6(1)(f))
You visit any pageTechnical server logs kept by the host: IP address, browser, pages requested, timeTo keep the site secure and working (legitimate interest, art. 6(1)(f))

Your cart is saved only in your own browser until you check out. It is not sent anywhere before then.

Who else handles it

Only services needed to run the studio, each bound by its own data protection terms:

  • Google (Sheets, Drive and Gmail): form requests, files and email are stored in our Google account.
  • Viva.com (Viva Payment Services S.A., Athens): processes payments.
  • Vercel: hosts the site and runs checkout.
  • Couriers: receive your name, address and phone to deliver your order.
  • Our accountant: receives order and invoice data for tax filings.

Your data is never sold or used for advertising.

Data outside the EU

Google and Vercel may process data in the United States. These transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Payments are processed by Viva.com inside the EU.

How long it is kept

  • Requests that do not lead to an order: deleted after 24 months.
  • Orders and invoices: kept as long as Greek tax law requires, usually 5 years after the end of the tax year.
  • Files you send for a job (models, photos): deleted 12 months after the job is finished, unless you ask us to keep them for reorders.
  • Server logs: kept by the host for a short period, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data held about you, correct it, delete it, limit how it is used, get a copy to take elsewhere, or object to its use. Write to ogcode3d@gmail.com and you will get an answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, Kifissias 1–3, 115 23 Athens, www.dpa.gr.

Students under 18

If a training student is under 18, a parent or guardian should send the request and be the contact for lessons.

Cookies

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Fonts and videos are served from this site itself, so visiting a page does not send your data to other companies.

The only thing stored on your device is your cart, in your browser's local storage, so it is still there when you come back. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
