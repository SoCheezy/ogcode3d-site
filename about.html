Behind the prints

OGCODE was founded and is run by Sotiris Vlogiannitis, mechanical engineer and AM specialist, a graduate of the National Technical University of Athens and a member of the Technical Chamber of Greece. His diploma research studied 3D printed stainless steel disks with internal lattice structures through finite element analysis, and he co-authored research on the friction behaviour of textured metallic surfaces.

As a consulting AM specialist, he has set up additive manufacturing facilities across Europe and Asia, in universities, research centres, hospitals and industrial production lines: from planning the space and everything it needs to run, to installing the machines, shaping the production workflow and training the teams behind it.

Through OGCODE he co-created Metachess, a generative art project that turns real chess games into sculpture and makes it possible for anyone to generate their own one-off piece. Metachess has appeared on a Times Square billboard, and its film Metachess: The Genesis is an official selection of the SIGGRAPH 2026 Electronic Theater.

With Zero Bricks, he is taking additive manufacturing to building scale: 3D printed architectural elements for construction and renovation, from interior reliefs to modular structures.

Beyond printing

The studio also teaches and builds. Lessons cover CAD, generative design, DfAM and 3D printing, one-to-one or in groups. When a project needs more than a part, we build the software around it: websites, shops, apps and tools such as the Start a print quote on this site or the Metachess generator. All the skills behind the studio, open to you.
