Who and what these terms cover

These terms apply to orders placed with Sotirios Vlogiannitis, trading as OGCODE. Full business details are in the legal notice. These terms cover products bought in the shop (Metachess sculptures and Bishop's Square chess sets) and, unless a written quote says otherwise, custom work, training and software development.

Orders and contract

You order by checking out in the cart and paying on Viva.com's secure page. The contract is made when you receive our order confirmation by email. If a piece cannot be made, you are told and refunded in full.

Prices and payment

Prices are in euros and include VAT where it applies. Shipping is shown in the cart before you pay and depends on the destination. Payment is taken by Viva.com at checkout, by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, IRIS (in Greece) or another method shown on the payment page.

Production and delivery

Every piece is made to order in Athens, so production starts after payment. You get an estimated ready date with your confirmation, and a tracking link when it ships.

We deliver to Greece, Cyprus and the European Union in two ways. By courier, sent once the piece is made. By the owner: the owner of OGCODE brings the piece to you by hand, in the Athens area or anywhere in Europe, on a date we agree with you. The fee for each option is shown before you pay. Delivery takes about one week in Greece and Cyprus and about two weeks elsewhere in Europe.

If a parcel arrives damaged, photograph it and the packaging and email us within 7 days. That helps settle the claim with the courier quickly. Your legal rights do not depend on it.

Right of withdrawal (14 days)

If you are a consumer in the EU and buy a standard shop piece (a Metachess library model or a Bishop's Square set), you can withdraw from the purchase within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason.

To withdraw, tell us clearly within those 14 days, for example by email to ogcode3d@gmail.com. You can use the model form below, but you do not have to. Send the piece back within 14 days of telling us. Return shipping is paid by you.

You get a refund of the price and the original courier shipping (fees for delivery by the owner are refunded only if the trip has not yet been made) within 14 days of your notice, to the same payment method. The refund may be held until the piece arrives back. If the piece is damaged by handling beyond what is needed to inspect it, the loss in value can be deducted.

Exception: the right of withdrawal does not apply to pieces made to your specifications or clearly personalised. This covers a Metachess sculpture made from your own game, custom designs, and parts printed from your files. This exception is in EU consumer law (Directive 2011/83/EU, art. 16(c)) and Greek law 2251/1994.

Legal guarantee

Every product comes with the 2-year legal guarantee of conformity under EU and Greek law. If something is faulty or not as described, email us with photos. You can have it repaired or replaced, or, if that is not possible, get a price reduction or a refund.

3D printed pieces show fine layer lines and small variations in finish from piece to piece. These are part of the process and are shown in the product photos. They are not defects.

Custom work and your files

  • Quotes are valid for 30 days unless they say otherwise. Work starts when you accept the quote and pay any deposit it asks for.
  • Your files and ideas stay yours. By sending a file or design you confirm you have the right to have it made. Jobs that would copy someone else's protected design or brand, break the law, or produce weapons or weapon parts are refused.
  • Confidentiality: your files are used only for your job and are not shared. Your work is shown in the portfolio only with your permission.
  • Designs created by OGCODE for you remain the studio's unless the quote says the files are handed over. You can always use the parts you receive as you wish.
  • Tolerances: sizes follow the tolerances of the chosen process, stated in the quote. For functional or load-bearing parts, you are responsible for checking that the part suits its final use, especially where safety is involved.

Training and software work

Training lessons can be moved free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice. Software projects follow the written proposal you accept. Where a quote or proposal says something different from these terms, the quote applies.

Liability

OGCODE is liable as the law provides. For business customers, liability for indirect losses (such as lost profit or downtime) is excluded and total liability is limited to the price of the order. Nothing here limits your rights as a consumer, or liability for intent, gross negligence or personal injury.

Complaints and disputes

If something goes wrong, write to ogcode3d@gmail.com and you will get an answer within 5 working days. If we cannot solve it together, consumers can turn to the Hellenic Consumer Ombudsman (Συνήγορος του Καταναλωτή), www.synigoroskatanaloti.gr, for out-of-court resolution.

These terms are governed by Greek law. Consumers keep the protection of the law of the country where they live. For business customers, the courts of Athens have jurisdiction.
